# Speed Rovers 2 — 1.0.3

## Presentation

Final direction: a modern motorsport showroom, balanced graphite panels, bright lime actions, angular surfaces and Rajdhani racing typography. Rider designs remain modest and family-friendly. The earlier white UI build (Codemagic #9) was cancelled before upload in response to feedback.

- Showroom home with animated framing, a prominent race action, equipped rider/city shortcuts and consistent bottom navigation.
- Five illustrated rider portraits and five city illustrations replace the legacy selection cards. Ownership, price and equipped state are explicit.
- Unlock confirmation shows price and wallet balance; insufficient funds cannot spend or equip. Purchases persist once and owned items can be equipped freely.
- Original procedural tap, screen-change and unlock sounds respect the effects setting. Animated track accents, button highlights, gallery drift, reward bursts and victory confetti add arcade feedback.
- Settings use labelled switches; pause offers race context and a leave-race confirmation.
- Results show actual finish position, elapsed time and banked coins, with a victory celebration and animated earnings reveal.
- A compact HUD replaces the oversized legacy pause artwork, with race progress, position, speed and gear.

Existing preference keys are retained. Driving simulation, opponent logic, collision recovery and track generation are unchanged.

## Validation

Native presentation review captures exercise home, collection, rider/city selection, settings, unlock confirmation, successful unlock, insufficient funds, HUD, pause, exit confirmation and first/third-place results. These are scripted UI states, not evidence of completing a race on an iPhone.

7,151 automated checks passed. The production unlock methods are covered for insufficient balance, locked-item equip rejection, exact spending, repeat-purchase idempotence, rider/city ownership isolation, free re-equipping and invalid indices. The existing racing regression suite also runs.

Device touch, safe-area and performance verification remains a TestFlight follow-up.


## Downtown visual slice

The first 4.8 km now use a continuous perspective surface shader, fading into the existing waterfront approach by 5.16 km. Road paint, intersections, work zones and sidewalks share the existing sprite projection. Derivative antialiasing removes horizontal strip stepping; mipmapped tiling asphalt controls distant texture shimmer. Four new original building facades replace the downtown building atlas. Vehicle suspension articulation preserves the tire ground anchor.

This is an incremental playable visual slice, not a claim of final photorealistic parity. Existing rider pose atlases, foliage, skyline and later districts remain and need further art-direction work. Phone frame rate and perceived motion must be assessed on the actual device.
