Speed Rovers 2 is a portrait motorcycle racer with responsive steering, busy roads and scenic routes. Race computer-controlled riders, weave past traffic and keep your momentum through every turn.

STEER YOUR WAY
Make a quick swipe to move sideways, or hold and drag your thumb for precise positioning between lanes. Automatic acceleration and gear changes let you focus on the road ahead.

SEVEN CHANGING ENVIRONMENTS
Ride through downtown streets, a sunset river bridge, green outskirts, a daytime city, the Italian coast, Tokyo at night and the Swiss Alps. Each stretch brings a different backdrop to the race.

RACE THROUGH THE TRAFFIC
Read the gaps, watch for oncoming vehicles and steer around roadworks and broken pavement. Contact slows you down and drops your momentum. Recover, accelerate and chase the riders ahead.

MAKE THE RIDE YOURS
Collect coins during races and use them to unlock riders and city selections. Your progress and settings are saved on your device.

HEAR THE ENGINE CLIMB
Engine pitch follows acceleration and gear changes, with wind, passing traffic and police sirens adding to the ride. Music and sound can be adjusted in Settings.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Choose the relaxed-pace option for a gentler ride. Play offline without creating an account.
