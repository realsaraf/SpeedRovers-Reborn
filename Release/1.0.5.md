# Speed Rovers 2: rider and traffic motion

- Shared 3.4-metre lane reference sizes cars, buses, player/rival/oncoming bikes, downtown buildings, trees and lamps consistently. Buses occupy about 75% of a lane rather than exceeding its width.
- Eight new transparent sprite masters from the built-in image generation tool, with recorded prompts in `motion-art-prompts.json`.
- Red and blue riders use sequential bank frames; green and orange use five bank angles across three speed-dependent postures. All use tire-anchored body/tread motion. The red atlas's final mirrored-exhaust frame is deliberately excluded.
- All twelve rear traffic identities and six oncoming identities use five camera angles, individual wheel/contact metadata, suspension and speed-dependent tire/rim motion.
- Traffic wheel phase integrates distance actually travelled, including stops, deceleration and oncoming motion.
- Region extraction records alpha bounds without altering the generated image pixels. Runtime rendering retains a single silhouette, without crossfading duplicate tires.

Validation: native Windows build, gameplay regression checks, 21 route captures, 18 vehicle identity captures, 12 rider turn captures, and an 18-second native gameplay sequence. These are desktop checks, not an iPhone performance or final-quality certification.

Remaining limitations: finite authored viewing angles remain; these are not fully rigged 3D models or an unlimited-direction animation system. Generated pose consistency still needs device play review. Existing menus, race handling, saved unlocks and environment visuals are preserved.
