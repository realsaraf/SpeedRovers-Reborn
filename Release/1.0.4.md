# Speed Rovers 2 — 1.0.4

- Extended the perspective surface shader to all seven environments, including sidewalk and curb coverage. Existing water/land rendering remains beneath the surface.
- Urban intersections and work-zone pavement follow the same route predicates as the simulation. Non-urban sections have no city crossings.
- Environment-weighted bridge highlights, Tokyo neon reflections and canopy shading transition with the scenery.
- Foliage uses a single stable sprite with continuous canopy deformation, anchored trunks, independently seeded wind and modest lighting variation. Removed crossfaded tree silhouettes and segmented tree sway.
- Soft underbody shadows complement tire-contact shadows. Bike body articulation is speed-dependent and no longer resizes the entire sprite for normal suspension movement.

## Validation

7,153 production regression checks passed, including shader presence and compiler diagnostics. Native route captures and an 18-second production-simulation motion sequence cover the visual changes. These checks do not establish iPhone frame rate or subjective visual acceptance.

## Remaining work

The existing nine-pose rider sets remain. Full articulated rider animation and replacement of repeated scenery with more authored environment variety are still needed. Water beyond the road is still rendered by the older strip path. This release is a further visual pass, not completion of the overall realism target.
