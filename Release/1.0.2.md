# Speed Rovers 2 — 1.0.2

## Gameplay
- All vehicle, roadwork, barrier and pothole impacts keep the race running.
- Impact severity uses obstacle size, rider speed and closing speed. The engine downshifts and accelerates gradually afterward.
- A 60 km race through seven environments replaces timer expiry. Three persistent competitors race to the same finish; results use their actual crossing times.
- The HUD shows elapsed race time and live placing. Time pickups and checkpoint messages no longer spawn.

## Presentation
- New original app icon and animated evening highway menu artwork.
- Redesigned home, rider/route selection, settings, pause and race results.
- Nine directional poses for rival riders, speed-driven wheel motion and subtle rider articulation.
- New three-angle front sprites for six oncoming vehicle types.
- Centered car roots and stronger tire/body contact shadows.
- Mirrored inward-facing bridge lamps, corrected light positions, ground shadows and cable anchor plates.
