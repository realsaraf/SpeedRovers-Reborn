Speed Rovers 2 audio refresh, source licenses verified 2026-10-14.

MUSIC
RaceScore.wav: Pure Raceway by MintoDog, 160 BPM, full 96-second loop.
https://opengameart.org/content/pure-raceway
MenuScore.wav: Racing Game Title by MintoDog, 140 BPM, full 60-second theme.
https://opengameart.org/content/racing-game-title
Both source pages explicitly dedicate their attached recordings under CC0 1.0.

RECORDED MOTORCYCLE
Engine.wav and EngineIdle.wav: processed excerpts of Motorcycle #0281 by
DenisChardonnet, provided by BigSoundBank under CC0 (public domain).
https://bigsoundbank.com/motorcycle-s0281.html
The original contains motorcycle startup, acceleration and shutdown. The game
uses trimmed, level-matched and crossfaded excerpts as idle/load loops.
Engine masters revised after phone feedback: reduce sub-bass with 220 Hz (load)
and 180 Hz (idle) high-pass filtering, gentle saturation to expose audible
midrange, and peak-controlled normalization. The original source had about
80-86 percent of its energy below 150 Hz and was easily masked by music on
small phone speakers. Original recording, source hash and CC0 license unchanged.

EFFECTS
Impact/Crash: layered metal/plate/glass recordings from Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
UI/Shift/Coin/Fuel/Jump/Pass/Finish: selections and edits from Kenney Interface Sounds 1.0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
Boost/BoostLoop: edits of thrusterFire_000 from Kenney Sci-fi Sounds 1.0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
All three packs contain a CC0 notice; exact notices are included alongside this file.
Wind: original deterministic band-limited airflow authored by the project.

PREPARATION AND AUDIT
Tools/import_licensed_ride_audio.py contains exact source URLs and edits.
LICENSED-AUDIO.json contains source SHA-256 hashes, individual input filenames,
processing descriptions, output hashes, durations, RMS, peaks and clipping counts.
CC0-1.0.txt contains the license legal code from Creative Commons.
Processed deliverables are PCM 16-bit 44.1 kHz; music is stereo. Unity compresses
music for streaming and short effects in memory at build time.

MIX AND PLAYBACK
The two composed themes crossfade on race/menu transitions. Music accompanies
the clearly audible engine/airflow bed; recorded idle and loaded engine layers follow RPM,
throttle and gear changes. Nitro has a sustained combustion layer. Impacts briefly
duck music. Saved music/effects choices remain respected through focus changes.
The native iOS playback session supports Silent Mode and mixes with user audio;
no microphone access or background-audio entitlement is requested.

These are freely reusable CC0 assets, not a claim that all audio on these sites
is free or that an acoustic phone/speaker review has already passed.
