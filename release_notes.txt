Speed Rovers 2 1.0.5

Shared lane-relative scaling corrects oversized cars/buses and undersized oncoming bikes.

Expanded directional artwork for the player, all rival rider styles and all traffic identities. Rear and oncoming traffic now have five viewing angles. Riders use denser bank/posture sheets with tire-aligned motion. One inconsistent red-bike extreme pose was excluded.

Wheels follow distance travelled rather than elapsed time times current speed, preventing phase jumps during slowing. Wheel masks and contact shadows follow each vehicle frame. Oncoming traffic uses new transparent artwork.

Please test rider turns, traffic passing, deceleration, stopped vehicle wheels and all rider selections. This remains a visual iteration; iPhone performance and final animation quality need device feedback.
