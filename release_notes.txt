Speed Rovers 2 1.0.2

Race three persistent opponents across a 60 km route. All collisions reduce speed according to obstacle size and closing speed, then the engine downshifts and gradually recovers. The race ends at the finish line instead of timer expiry.

New app icon, animated home screen and redesigned menus. Nine lean poses for rival riders, moving wheels, directional oncoming vehicles, grounded car shadows and inward-facing bridge lamps.

Swipe left or right to steer. Please test collision recovery, race placing, rival turns, traffic grounding, bridge lamps, menu animation and frame rate on your iPhone or iPad.
