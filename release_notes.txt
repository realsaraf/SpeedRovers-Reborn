Speed Rovers 2 1.0.4

Visual refinement across the entire race: continuous perspective asphalt, antialiased road markings and curbs in all seven locations, warm bridge road highlights, Tokyo neon reflections and restrained canopy shading.

Trees and shrubs now use continuous wind motion with one solid silhouette, replacing the ghosted crossfading and segmented sway. Seeded tree variation and less repetitive shrub placement improve the roadside. Soft vehicle ground shadows retain dark tire contact; rider upper-body movement is stronger while tire contact remains anchored.

Please test full-route transitions, Tokyo reflections, bridge sidewalks, tree motion and vehicle contact on your iPhone. Existing rider pose atlases remain; this build does not introduce a new multi-frame rider animation set. Unlocks and settings are preserved.
