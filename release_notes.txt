Speed Rovers 2 2.1.2 - Engine, Fuel and Durability
Engine sound remastered for phone speakers, with louder acceleration and idle layers, immediate startup and recovery after interrupted playback. Background music remains.
Removed road coins. Fuel cans now have glowing rotating arrows for advance visibility. Fuel stops are 5.6 km apart, restore 30%, and do not appear just before the finish. Race and close-pass credits still unlock bikes.
The bike tolerates 50% more cumulative impact damage, while collisions still slow it down and clean riding repairs damage.
Please test engine audibility on your iPhone at idle, acceleration and top speed, then after pausing or locking/unlocking. Also check fuel marker visibility and the more forgiving collision balance.
