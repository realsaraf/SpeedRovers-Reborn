Speed Rovers 2 2.0.0 — landscape cockpit racing
A new first-person race experience with hold-left braking and hold-right acceleration. Drag while holding the throttle to steer, or choose tilt steering in Settings. Race three rivals through longer downtown, riverside and garden districts. Collisions reduce speed and gears; they do not immediately end the race. Collect red fuel cans, jump for gold coins, and activate rechargeable nitro at 50% charge or higher. Nitro stays grounded. New home, route selection, garage upgrades, settings, pause and results screens. Fresh progression starts with this version.
Please test landscape display, simultaneous throttle/brake/steering touches, audio, soft haptics, installation and sustained performance. Rival art and traffic variety remain work in progress.
