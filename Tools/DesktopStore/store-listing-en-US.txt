SHARED DESCRIPTION
Take the rider's seat in Speed Rovers 2: Highway Racing, a first-person arcade motorcycle racer for desktop.

Accelerate, brake and steer freely across the road as you chase three computer-controlled rivals through moving traffic. Time a nitro boost to make your pass, watch your fuel and follow the roadside pickup beacons to keep the race alive.

Choose Grand Tour, Coast to Summit or Storm Chaser: three predefined routes spanning 28, 32 and 36 kilometres. Travel through city streets, a suspension crossing, coastal roads, stormy mountain passes, tunnels and wooded valleys, with changing light and weather along the course.

Collisions slow the bike and damage its condition. Ride clean to recover; repeated heavy impacts can end your race. Earn in-game credits from race finishes and close passes, then unlock the Vector R and Phantom RS performance upgrades. Save your best time on each route and try again.

Play fullscreen or in a resizable window. Use keyboard controls or the onscreen throttle, brake, jump and nitro controls, with separate music and sound-effects settings.

KEY FEATURES
- First-person cockpit racing against three computer-controlled riders.
- Three long, predefined routes: 28 km, 32 km and 36 km.
- City, bridge, coast, mountain, tunnel and forest scenery.
- Continuous steering, progressive acceleration and automatic gear changes.
- Recharging nitro boosts, a jump ability and marked fuel pickups.
- Collision damage with recovery during clean riding.
- Earned credits, three bike performance options and saved route records.
- Fullscreen/windowed switching and keyboard controls for desktop play.

KEYBOARD CONTROLS
W or Up Arrow: accelerate
S or Down Arrow: brake
A/D or Left/Right Arrows: steer
Space: jump
Left Shift or N: nitro
Escape or P: pause/resume

WINDOWS DISPLAY CONTROL
F11 or Alt+Enter: switch fullscreen/windowed
The same switch is available in Settings. Display choice is saved.

MAC DISPLAY CONTROL
