# Speed Rovers Reborn beta privacy

Effective October 13, 2026. This page applies to the Unity 2.0.0 TestFlight beta.

The game saves coins, unlocked riders and cities, preferences and best distance locally on your device. This progress is not sent to a game server or synced between devices. Removing the app may remove its saved progress.

This beta has no account sign-in, advertising, real-money purchases or online leaderboard service. Unity Analytics, Ads and cloud diagnostic reporting are disabled in this project's settings. The game does not request your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Apple's TestFlight service separately handles beta invitations, installation and update delivery, tester feedback and diagnostics under Apple's terms and privacy practices. Feedback you send through TestFlight, or by email, may include information that you choose to provide. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information in feedback.

For questions or beta feedback, contact realsaraf@gmail.com. We use messages you send to respond and investigate the reported issue.

If future versions add online features or change how information is handled, this page will be updated for that version.
